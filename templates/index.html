How it works

Three steps, fully automated

1

Paste the link

Drop any Mediaspace or Kaltura video page URL into the box above.

2

We find the stream

A headless browser opens the page, clicks play, and intercepts the M3U8 video stream URL from the network traffic.

3

Download & stitch

Every video segment is downloaded in parallel, then stitched into a single MP4 with ffmpeg. One click to save.

Mediaspace Downloader — runs entirely on your own machine, nothing is uploaded.
